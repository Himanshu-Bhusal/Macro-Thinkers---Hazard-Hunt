Privacy Policy

How Macro Thinker handles information shared through this website.

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Information we collect

When you contact us or request a demo, we may collect your name, company, work email address, company size, and the details you choose to include in your message.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, arrange demonstrations, improve Hazard Hunt, and communicate about a request you have made. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing and retention

We may use service providers that help us operate the website or receive enquiries. They may process information only to provide those services. We keep enquiry information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it by emailing himanshubhusal62@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or our information practices change. The date above shows when it was last revised.
