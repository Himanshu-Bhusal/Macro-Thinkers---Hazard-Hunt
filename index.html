The Problem

Why Signing a Sheet Isn't Enough

Posters, PDFs, toolbox talks

The current routine is a handout, a poster on the wall, a five-minute talk before a shift starts.

Attendance isn't understanding

Workers sign the sheet. That tells you who showed up, not who can actually spot a hazard on the floor.

Risk stays invisible until it isn't

A blocked aisle or a damaged pallet nobody flags in training gets found the hard way, during a shift or an inspection.
