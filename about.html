About Us

A small team, one real client, one tool built properly.

We're not a big vendor with a hundred features. We're a team that sat down with a real warehouse's health and safety lead and built one thing that fixes one problem.

Who We Are

Macro Thinker

Macro Thinker builds practical, browser-based training tools for industries where safety training hasn't kept up with how the work actually happens on the ground. Hazard Hunt is the first of these — built around a real client's warehouse, not a generic scenario, and tested against their actual near-miss log rather than a textbook hazard list.

“We'd rather build one tool that genuinely fixes a problem a real safety officer described to us than a dozen features nobody asked for.”

Why This Problem

Hazard Perception Was the One Real Gap

We looked at three possible training topics before settling on this one. Fire safety is already adequately covered through most sites' insurers. Manual handling is usually already taught hands-on, on site. Hazard perception was the gap: nothing currently tests whether someone can actually notice a hazard while they're moving around a real space — only whether they sat through a talk about one.

Built for the floor, not the boardroom.
